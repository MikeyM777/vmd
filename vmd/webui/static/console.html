Built-in detector

Our own motion gate and AI. Runs on the streams selected below. Tunable, and every decision is logged with a reason.

Camera analytics

The camera's own detection, received as events over its API. Untested — availability will be confirmed when the camera is reachable.

Detection

Alarm on anything that moves. Wind, rain, foliage and birds are suppressed by track filters, not by the AI — the AI only labels what it can, and never blocks an alarm it cannot explain.

thermal
visible

AI is off for thermal by default: at 700 m a person is about 13 px there, too few for identification. Movement is still detected — it just arrives unlabelled.

Camera

Field of view is measured on site rather than assumed: point the thermal camera at an object of known size and distance, and the software derives the lens angle.

Streams

The RTSP addresses the camera serves. Nothing is recorded until at least one is filled in and ticked.

If you do not know the paths, run uv run python spike/probe_camera.py <address> — it asks the camera and prints the ones that answer.

Judged for this site: two thermal and visible PTZ cameras on one radio link, an air-gapped desktop, one operator. A feature that is excellent in a shopping centre can be worthless here, and a few are impossible on a machine with no network. Those are marked ✕ Not for us and say why rather than being left out.

Finding things that matter

  • Areas to ignore

    Paint out the parts of the picture that are never news - a road, a treeline, a flag.

    Here: Freehand, drawn on a still of what the camera sees, with no numbers anywhere. Rescales itself if the stream resolution changes.

    Who has it: Everyone. Frigate, Milestone, Blue Iris, SightLogix.

    ●In VMD

  • Areas to watch★

    The opposite: alarm ONLY inside a drawn area, and stay silent everywhere else.

    Here: The single biggest false-alarm cut available. Right now everything outside the ignore areas is watched, so an area of interest has to be built by painting out the whole rest of the world.

    Who has it: Frigate (zones), Axis Perimeter Defender, Genetec, Milestone.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Tripwire, with a direction★

    A line across the picture. Alarm when something crosses it, and only one way if you say so.

    Here: This is what a perimeter IS. "Someone crossed the fence line inward" is a different fact from "something moved", and it is the one worth waking up for.

    Who has it: Axis, Genetec, Milestone, Hanwha, most cameras themselves.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Must stay in the area for N seconds

    Ignore anything that only clips the edge of a zone in passing.

    Here: Cheap on top of areas to watch, and kills the branch that swings across a corner of the frame.

    Who has it: Frigate calls it inertia; Axis and Genetec call it dwell.

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Loitering★

    Alarm when something stays put for longer than it should.

    Here: Somebody standing still at 700 m is exactly what movement detection is worst at: they stop moving and the track dies.

    Who has it: Axis, Genetec, SightLogix, Hanwha.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Speed and direction filters

    Too fast is a vehicle, too slow is drift, and the wrong bearing is not your problem.

    Here: There is a travel rule (the wind rule) but no speed and no bearing. Bearing would let the far side of the road be ignored without painting it out.

    Who has it: SightLogix, Axis, Genetec.

    ◐Partly a few days

  • Size in metres, not pixels★

    Calibrate the view once, then say "ignore anything smaller than a person at that range".

    Here: The size rule is in pixels and a share of frame height today, which means it changes meaning every time the camera zooms. A rabbit at 40 m and a man at 700 m are the same number of pixels.

    Who has it: SightLogix builds their whole product on this (georegistration).

    ○Not yet a week or more

  • Human-shaped filter

    A person is taller than wide. Reject blobs that are not.

    Here: Very cheap, and it throws away a lot of what a treeline produces.

    Who has it: SightLogix (aspect ratio filtering).

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Steady the picture before looking at it

    Take the mast sway out of the frame so wind does not read as movement.

    Here: A pole at 700 m moves in wind, and every pixel moves with it. This is the difference between usable and unusable on a windy night.

    Who has it: SightLogix stabilise in the camera before analytics run.

    ○Not yet a week or more

  • Say what it was - person, vehicle, animal

    A model puts a noun on what moved.

    Here: You asked for this removed and the arithmetic agrees: at 700 m a person is about 13 pixels, and a model trained on photographs has nothing to say about that. The code is still there behind one switch if you change your mind.

    Who has it: Frigate, Axis, SightLogix, Genetec, Milestone.

    ✕Not for us

  • Two levels: worth waking for, and worth logging★

    Some movement is an alarm, the rest is just a record.

    Here: Everything is equal now. A tripwire crossing and a bird are the same event with the same sound.

    Who has it: Frigate (alerts vs detections).

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Different rules at different times of day

    Arm at dusk, relax during working hours, tighten at night.

    Here: The perimeter is not the same problem at 14:00 and 03:00, and today one setting has to serve both.

    Who has it: Blue Iris (profiles and schedules), Milestone, Genetec.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Sound detection

    Alarm on a gunshot, glass, a shout.

    Here: There is no audio anywhere in this system on purpose: the camera sends a format MP4 cannot hold, and it cost a day of recording once already.

    Who has it: Frigate (600+ sounds), Genetec.

    ✕Not for us

Knowing the camera is still telling the truth

  • Tamper alarm - covered, turned, sprayed, defocused★

    Alarm when the VIEW changes, not when something moves in it.

    Here: A flat or frozen picture is already noticed and reported as blindness, and a camera that has been moved suppresses its own frame. Neither of them raises anything the operator hears. This is the attack a perimeter system has to survive.

    Who has it: Genetec, Milestone, Axis, Hanwha.

    ◐Partly a few days

  • Was it recording all night?★

    A report of every gap in the recording, with the reason.

    Here: The segment index already knows. Nothing asks it. "Yes, all of it" is the question that gets asked the morning after something happened, and today the only answer is scrolling the Logs tab.

    Who has it: Milestone, Genetec, Nx Witness.

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Show the rules that are throwing things away★

    How many blobs each rejection rule discarded: too small, above the horizon, inside an ignore area.

    Here: These counters are already computed and published, and nothing displays them. A size rule set wrong deletes every real detection and says nothing - and on FHD the standard sensitivity may be doing exactly that.

    Who has it: Nobody shows this well. Frigate's debug view comes closest.

    ◐Partly a day or so

  • Health at a glance

    One page: every camera, recording, disk, link, up or down.

    Here: The status band across the top, plus the Logs tab. Smaller than theirs and it fits two cameras.

    Who has it: Milestone, Genetec, Nx Witness all sell this hard.

    ●In VMD

  • Disk failure warning, not just disk full

    SMART attributes, write errors, a drive on its way out.

    Here: Free space and write failures are watched. The drive's own opinion of itself is not asked for.

    Who has it: Milestone, Genetec.

    ◐Partly a few days

  • Clock sanity

    Warn when the machine's time is wrong, because every recording is filed under it.

    Here: This machine has no internet and its clock is set by hand. The code already survives the clock going backwards; nothing warns anybody that it did.

    Who has it: Milestone, Genetec (NTP monitoring).

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Redundant recording / failover server

    A second machine takes over when the first dies.

    Here: One box, air-gapped. A second one is a procurement decision and not a feature.

    Who has it: Milestone, Genetec, Nx Witness.

    ✕Not for us

What the operator does when something happens

  • A sound

    Make a noise in the room.

    Here: Two alternating tones, purpose-built so it is not mistaken for a Windows notification, and it can be switched off.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ●In VMD

  • The picture of what moved, with the box on it

    A still of the exact frame the detection was confirmed on.

    Here: In the side column, needing no dismissing, and saved to disk so it can be sent to somebody.

    Who has it: Frigate (snapshots), Milestone, Genetec.

    ●In VMD

  • An alarm list with a state on each one

    New, acknowledged, closed - with who and when.

    Here: You had the red strip removed and were right to. What a list gives that the strip did not is history: the ten things that happened while nobody was in the room.

    Who has it: Every serious VMS.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Silence one camera for an hour★

    Time-limited, and it un-silences itself.

    Here: The honest answer to a windy night. Today the only lever is switching detection off, which stays off.

    Who has it: Genetec (shelving), most alarm systems.

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Escalate if nobody responds

    Nobody acknowledged in N minutes - do something louder.

    Here: Only worth it with somewhere to escalate TO. On an air-gapped box that means a louder sound or a relay.

    Who has it: Genetec Mission Control, most alarm platforms.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • A checklist on the alarm

    What to do about this, written down, at the moment it happens.

    Here: The user guide covers this. On screen at 03:40 is better than in a PDF.

    Who has it: Genetec (action plans), Milestone.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Mark a moment by hand

    Bookmark something you noticed, so you can find it later.

    Here: The timeline only shows what the detector found. There is no way to mark something you saw with your own eyes.

    Who has it: Milestone, Nx Witness, Genetec.

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Notify a phone

    Push, SMS, email.

    Here: Impossible and deliberately so. This machine has no network of any kind, and that is a requirement of the site.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ✕Not for us

Driving the camera

  • Steer by keys and by dragging the picture

    Point the camera at something.

    Here: Arrow keys, Shift for fine, drag near an edge to slew, and a zoom bar per lens on a shared gimbal.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ●In VMD

  • Saved positions★

    Store "the gate", "the north fence", jump back to them in one click.

    Here: There is a Home and nothing else. Nine tenths of the steering an operator does is going back to the same three places.

    Who has it: Everyone, including the camera itself.

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Go back on its own★

    After nobody has touched it for N minutes, return to the position that matters.

    Here: The failure this prevents: somebody looks at something at 02:00, walks away, and the perimeter is unwatched until morning with nothing on the screen saying so.

    Who has it: Everyone (autoguard / return to home).

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Patrol between positions

    Tour the saved views on a timer.

    Here: Worth having, worth thinking about: a camera on patrol is not watching the other 90% of its cycle, and movement detection is fighting a moving background the whole time.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Follow what it found

    The camera tracks the moving thing by itself.

    Here: Impressive and hard. It also takes the camera off the perimeter to chase a rabbit, and there is no second camera covering while it does.

    Who has it: Axis (licensed extra), Hanwha, Genetec.

    ○Not yet a week or more

  • Point at what the other camera found

    One sensor detects, the other slews to it and zooms in.

    Here: You have exactly the right hardware for this: thermal finds it, visible goes and looks. It is the most valuable hard thing on this list.

    Who has it: SightLogix, Thermal Radar, Axis - the standard perimeter pattern.

    ○Not yet a week or more

  • Zoom into the picture without moving the camera

    Digital zoom on the live view.

    Here: Optical zoom moves the lens and takes the whole view with it. Digital zoom lets one operator look closer while the recording keeps the wide view.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Brightness and contrast on the thermal

    Stretch the picture so a warm thing stands out from a cold field.

    Here: A thermal picture at 03:00 in winter is a grey rectangle with a slightly less grey rectangle in it.

    Who has it: FLIR's own tools, Hanwha, Genetec.

    ○Not yet a few days

Recordings and evidence

  • Record everything, all the time

    Continuous recording rather than only on movement.

    Here: Continuous, segmented, with an index and automatic deletion of the oldest. Stronger than the event-only default, because it holds the ten seconds BEFORE anybody noticed.

    Who has it: All of them offer it; many home systems default to event-only.

    ●In VMD

  • Protect a recording from being deleted★

    Lock the night that matters so retention cannot reclaim it.

    Here: The most serious gap on this page. Retention deletes the oldest footage to stay inside the budget, and it will happily delete the incident somebody is going to ask about next week. Nothing can currently stop it.

    Who has it: Milestone call it Evidence Lock and sell it as a tier upgrade.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Export a clip

    Cut a piece out and save it to a file.

    Here: Marked on the timeline and saved out. On the Playback tab, which is switched off by default now.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ●In VMD

  • Prove the clip was not edited

    A signature or hash so an export can be shown to somebody who has to trust it.

    Here: If footage from this system is ever going to matter to anybody outside the room, it needs to arrive with something that says it was not altered.

    Who has it: Milestone (signed exports), Genetec.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Search the recording for movement in one spot

    Draw a box on the past and find every time something moved inside it.

    Here: The one investigation tool that genuinely saves hours. It needs the Playback tab to be on.

    Who has it: Milestone Smart Search, Nx Witness, Genetec.

    ○Not yet a week or more

  • Thumbnails while scrubbing

    See where you are on the timeline without waiting for video to load.

    Here: Playback is off by default now, so this is only worth it if it comes back into use.

    Who has it: Frigate (previews), Nx Witness, Milestone.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Both cameras in step

    Play two recordings from the same moment side by side.

    Here: Both-together on the Playback tab, sharing one clock.

    Who has it: Milestone, Genetec, Nx Witness.

    ●In VMD

  • Save a still from the live picture

    One button, one JPEG of what is on screen right now.

    Here: Stills are saved automatically when something moves. There is no way to grab one because you want one.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ○Not yet a day or so

  • Who did what, and when

    An audit trail of operator actions, separate from the technical log.

    Here: The Logs tab records what the SYSTEM did. Nothing records that somebody turned detection off at 21:40.

    Who has it: Milestone, Genetec - usually for compliance reasons.

    ○Not yet a few days

The link, the disk, the machine

  • Match the picture to what the radio can carry

    Turn the camera's bitrate down when the link is full, and back up when it is not.

    Here: This is VMD's own, and it exists because this link is the binding constraint. It reads airtime rather than megabits, because airtime is what a wireless link runs out of.

    Who has it: Nobody does this. Milestone and Genetec monitor bandwidth; none of them drive the camera from it.

    ●In VMD

  • Show the radio link

    Signal, airtime, throughput, and per-camera on a shared access point.

    Here: One word, two bars, and every sentence behind a toggle. It found the fault that was freezing one of your pictures.

    Who has it: Nobody. A VMS shows you the camera is offline, not why.

    ●In VMD

  • Run with no internet at all

    Install, update and operate a machine that has never been on a network.

    Here: One folder, one zip, no runtime downloads, and three separate outbound-traffic bugs found and closed.

    Who has it: Most are cloud-connected by default. Frigate and ZoneMinder can run offline; the enterprise ones assume a licence server.

    ●In VMD

  • Trigger a light or a siren

    Close a relay when something crosses the line.

    Here: The most effective perimeter response there is, and it needs a contact closure - the camera's own alarm output, or a USB relay. Worth deciding before the next site visit.

    Who has it: Everyone, through the camera's own output or an I/O module.

    ○Not yet needs hardware

  • Tell another system

    ONVIF events, or a message on a wire, so a control room knows.

    Here: There is nothing else on this network, because there is no network.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ✕Not for us

  • Users, passwords, roles

    Different people see and do different things.

    Here: One machine, in a locked room, one operator. Passwords are shown in plain text on purpose here, and that is the right call for this site rather than a shortcut.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ✕Not for us

  • Blackout regions in the recording

    Privacy masks that are burnt into what is stored.

    Here: Only matters if the camera can see somewhere it should not. Worth knowing whether it can.

    Who has it: Everyone, usually for a legal reason.

    ○Not yet a few days

  • Back up the settings★

    One file, restorable, so a rebuild is minutes.

    Here: It is all in settings.json and the areas you drew are in it. Nothing copies it anywhere, so a disk failure takes the setup with it.

    Who has it: Everyone.

    ◐Partly a day or so
